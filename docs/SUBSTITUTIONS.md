# Substitutions (original stand-ins for third-party media)

## Login scene
None of the video's five user pictures is a Windows default picture (see assets-src/SOURCES.md
section 5), so every tile uses a Microsoft-shipped Windows Vista account picture
(assets-src/user-pictures/vista-128), shown as the blue duotone in the bar and in colour on hover
and on the slab.

| Where | In the video | Stand-in |
|---|---|---|
| Sondra (tile, slab) | custom close-up photo of a red/yellow daisy | Vista "User" (orange gerbera), cropped so the flower centre sits left of middle like the video's picture; the slab material washes it to the video's pastel look |
| Tommy | custom photo of a brown-and-white dog | Vista "Usertile6" (golden retriever) |
| Jenny | photo of a crying baby | Vista "Usertile3" (goldfish), the closest bright, high-key tile |
| Kelly | Totoro plush (film character) | Vista "Usertile5" (sports balls) |
| Angela | photo of a woman's face | Vista "Usertile8" (kitten close-up) |

Not substitutions: the XP flag + "Microsoft Windows" lockup and the VAIO logo are the real marks
(vector, recoloured to the video's pastel/white styling).

## my Music window
All covers are generated by `tools/music/covers.py` from simple shapes (no photos, faces,
logos or lettering; "type" is drawn as soft bars), blended over a 5x5 field of each reference
tile's mean colours so the grid reads like the video at a glance. Files: `public/img/music/covers/`.

| Where | In the video | Stand-in |
|---|---|---|
| Album grid, 32 tiles (22 distinct covers, some repeated as in the video) | real album covers (photos of artists, band logos, artwork) | `a_white` ... `v_blue`: original abstract compositions echoing each tile's colours and layout (e.g. three stacked lamps on black, a cyan sky over a dark valley, four faceless figures on cyan, a blue/orange wheel) |
| Grid tile row 2 column 3 and the 3D album slab on the album page | Tweet "Southern Hummingbird" cover (photo of the artist on gold) | `k_tweet` / `tweet-lg`: gold foil band fading into grey with dark sweeping ribbons and a drawn hummingbird; the large version is fitted to the album face's 6x6 colour field (`tools/music/tweet_field.json`) so the gold-and-grey slab reads like the video |

Not substitutions: the toolbar, task pane and header icons are redrawn Microsoft UI art
(SVG in `src/scenes/music/icons.jsx`); the Folders and Find icons use the XP folder and
search icons from `assets-src/icons` (`public/img/music/folder.png`, `find.png`).

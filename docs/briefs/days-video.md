# Second reference: "Longhorn Days Unreleased Video 01" (user-supplied inspiration)

YouTube HFTqOzKaSl8 (UXEvangelist, 2009, a capture of the same Longhorn Plex flash demo family,
98 s, 640x480). Local copy (gitignored): `ref2/days01.mp4`, all frames `ref2/full/fNNNNN.jpg`
(30 fps, 1-based), 1 fps sheets `ref2/sheets/`. The video has a "www.uxevangelist.net"
watermark bottom right and added music: ignore both (no audio, no watermark).

It shows more of the same OS, in the same chrome. Use it as the **design source** for the
corresponding parts of our OS (positions/sizes scale by 960/640 = 1.5 to our stage). Content
rules from os.md still apply: no photos of real people (it shows family photos, a baby, people
on Hurricane Ridge); use original stand-ins or neutral generated landscape-style images, and list
them in docs/SUBSTITUTIONS.md. Names and captions it shows may be kept or made neutral.

What it shows (frame numbers at 30 fps):
- 1-30: the desktop with a different wallpaper (green tree frog on a leaf) and the same sidebar,
  user "Susan Victor" in the sidebar header. (We keep Stonehenge as default; Display Properties
  may offer a green "leaf" stand-in wallpaper, no frog photo.)
- 40-75: AutoPlay-style dialog "Sony Digital Camera: Transfer pictures and video to your computer.
  Which provider do you want to use?" with two options (Sony Digital Camera / Windows Picture and
  Video Acquisition), "Always use the selected provider" checkbox, Cancel. (f00054)
- 78-290: **Windows Picture and Video Acquisition** wizard (window title bar, big framed photo at
  top-left, "Welcome / Pictures and Video Acquisition" in the my-Music-style blue title, Auto
  Capture / Manual Capture radios, "Remove video and picture from camera once copied" checkbox,
  Next / Cancel). Then "Capture in Progress": currently copying, location, title, progress bars,
  time remaining, a preview tile where each copied picture flies in with 3D motion (f00126-f00270).
  (f00102, f00198)
- 290-520: "Please name and describe these events": thumbnails fly in and arrange into a grid,
  Event 1 / Event 2 tabs, name field, date, keywords checklist (Anniversary, Baby, Birthday,
  Family, Friends, Favorites, Party, Vacation, + new), voice annotation, Done / Cancel. (f00366)
- 530-700: **my Pictures & Videos** explorer (same window as my Music): header with a 3D photo
  frame device, "Windows my Pictures & Videos" title, View Slide Show / Create Album / Burn DVD;
  task pane "enjoy pictures" (View Slide show, Create an Album, Burn DVD, Share Pictures, Print
  Pictures), "find pictures" (Containing the words field, Show all images by: Ella, Parties,
  Weekend trips, More...), "Refine By Date" with a mini calendar (2002 April, days with pictures
  highlighted), "Of this file type: Pictures, Videos, Albums"; content = date-grouped thumbnail
  rows ("April 2, 2002 Monday | Jee Eun @ Pike Place..."), range/view-by/size bar. (f00600)
  Zooming the calendar out (month grid, then year grid) changes the thumbnail density (f00624-666).
- 700-830: a full-screen blue **10-foot view** ("my Pictures & Videos" huge faded watermark text,
  left nav "find pictures: albums, presets, folders, sort by date, sort by name, sort by type",
  a 3x3 grid of captioned photos "Hurricane Ridge May, 2000 / Hurricane 001..."). (f00760)
- 830-1130: photo **detail view** inside the explorer: big photo, title "Hurricane Ridge 5",
  Comments, Keywords, Location, Stored in, Creator, Size, Date Created, Edit button, previous/next

- 1500-1720: **Create an album** window: three columns "1. Find >", "2. Choose images >" (checkbox

- 1840: an MSN/ofoto web page in IE (we show the offline page instead).
- 1860-2780: the my Music window as in our video.
- 2790-2860: **Log off**: the desktop cuts/fades to the login screen (aurora, tiles), then the
  screen fades to black (f02775-f02860): use as the log-off / shut-down reference.
